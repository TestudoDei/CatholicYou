# CatholicYou editorial standard

Every published entry should help a reader pray while remaining precise about what the Church teaches and what history can establish.

## Required fields

- Name, slug, feast date, calendar scope, liturgical rank, short summary
- Historical notes and devotional traditions kept distinct
- Sources with publisher, title, URL or publication data, and access date
- Editorial status and reviewer

## Review outcomes

- **Approved:** Claims are supported, terminology is sound, and devotional language is proportionate.
- **Approved with edits:** The entry is usable after listed corrections.
- **Requires human review:** Sources conflict, a quotation cannot be traced, the subject concerns private revelation, indulgences, moral theology, controversy, or apparent Church authority.

## Preferred sources

Prefer Scripture, Vatican documents, the Roman Missal and liturgical calendars, bishops’ conferences, critical editions, and reputable Catholic reference works. Avoid using unsourced devotional aggregators as factual authorities.
